Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a password. When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not ask for bank account or card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and is accessible only through your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may delete your expense entries at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may send a request to Google when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
